# 3 Days Merzouga SEO Strategy

## Site Purpose

3 Days Merzouga should rank as a specialist Morocco desert tour site for travelers comparing Marrakech to Merzouga, Marrakech to Fes via Merzouga, and 3-4 day Sahara itineraries. The commercial goal is simple: get qualified travelers to choose a tour page, understand the route and price, then contact the team by form, email, or WhatsApp.

The site should avoid looking like a generic Morocco travel blog. Its strongest positioning is narrow expertise: Marrakech pickup, Merzouga and Erg Chebbi desert camps, Dades Valley, Todra Gorge, Ait Ben Haddou, private tours, shared tours, luxury camp upgrades, and clear group-size pricing.

## Competitor Pattern

Ranking sites in this niche usually fall into three groups:

1. Large marketplaces: GetYourGuide, Viator, TourRadar, Tripadvisor.
2. Local tour companies: Morocco Desert Trips, Marrakech Desert Trips, Morocco Fabulous Travel, Sahara Desert Tour operators.
3. Informational travel guides: destination guides about Merzouga, Erg Chebbi, Sahara camps, Ait Ben Haddou, and Marrakech desert trips.

Common winning page elements:

- Exact-match commercial titles such as "3 Day Marrakech to Merzouga Desert Tour".
- Route-first page structure: Marrakech, Ait Ben Haddou, Dades Valley, Todra Gorge, Merzouga, Erg Chebbi, return or Fes drop-off.
- Day-by-day itinerary with driving expectations.
- Clear inclusions and exclusions.
- Price, availability, private/shared options, and camp upgrade information.
- FAQs targeting search objections: Is 3 days enough? Is Merzouga worth it? Shared vs private? What to pack? How long is the drive?
- Review signals, local operator trust, WhatsApp/contact speed, and cancellation/payment clarity.
- Internal links from blog guides to exact commercial tour pages.

3 Days Merzouga can compete by being more focused, clearer, and more transparent than broad marketplaces while still matching their intent coverage.

## URL Rules

- Keep URLs short, lowercase, and keyword-readable.
- Use one canonical page per intent. Do not create `/3-days-merzouga-tour`, `/merzouga-3-day-tour`, and `/3-day-desert-tour` as separate competing pages.
- Keep commercial pages under `/tours/`.
- Keep educational guides under `/blog/`.
- Keep evergreen place pages under `/destinations/`.
- Avoid date-based URLs unless the page is truly about a specific year.

## On-Page SEO Template For Tour Pages

Every tour page should include:

- H1 with exact route and duration.
- First paragraph containing route, duration, start city, end city, Erg Chebbi, and camp experience.
- Short decision block: best for, duration, route, starts/ends, private/shared, price from.
- Day-by-day itinerary with real stop names and realistic drive-time notes.
- Included and excluded sections.
- Price table and booking widget.
- Camp options: standard, luxury, private tent, bathroom expectations.
- Transport details: air-conditioned vehicle, driver language, pickup area.
- FAQs with 6-10 questions unique to that route.
- Review/trust block with real review count and source once available.
- Internal links to sibling tour pages and supporting guides.
- JSON-LD: TouristTrip, Offer, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, TravelAgency.

## Internal Linking Strategy

Use the 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour page as the main commercial target.

- Home links to `/tours/3-day-marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour` with anchor "3-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour".
- `/tours` links to all three tour pages with descriptive anchors.
- Blog pages link upward to the most relevant tour page in the first half of the article.
- Destination pages link to every tour that visits that destination.
- Tour pages link sideways:
  - 3-day Merzouga page links to 4-day tour for slower pace.
  - 3-day Merzouga page links to Marrakech-Fes page for travelers not returning to Marrakech.
  - Marrakech-Fes page links to Ziz Valley and Fes-route guide.
  - 4-day page links to family/private/luxury camp content.

## Technical SEO Checklist

- Keep `sitemap.ts`, but update it whenever new pages are added.
- Use real `lastModified` dates instead of one fixed date for every URL when possible.
- Keep canonical URLs absolute through `metadataBase`.
- Add `robots.ts` sitemap reference, already done.
- Ensure every important page is linked from navigation, footer, tour cards, or contextual content.
- Use server-rendered text for key content, not content hidden behind client-only widgets.
- Compress and self-host key hero images where possible instead of relying only on remote Unsplash URLs.
- Add descriptive alt text to every route image.
- Use 1200x630 OG images per tour and ideally unique images per route.
- Track Core Web Vitals after launch, especially LCP on hero images.
- Validate schema with Google's Rich Results Test.
- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.

## Content Quality Requirements

Google rewards content made for users, not content written only to attract clicks. For this niche, that means every page should answer practical booking questions with first-hand, route-specific detail:

- Exact route and overnight locations.
- Honest drive-time expectations.
- Who the tour is best for and who should choose another option.
- Clear pricing logic.
- Real camp and vehicle information.
- Local safety, seasonal, and packing advice.
- Original photos when possible.
- Real reviews and business details.

Avoid generic AI travel filler like "discover magical Morocco" unless it is backed by specific route detail.

## Authority And Trust

Priorities before heavy link building:

- Add real business name, address/service area, phone, WhatsApp, email, and social profiles.
- Replace placeholder social links with real profiles.
- Add genuine Tripadvisor or Google review links and review count if allowed by platform rules.
- Add author/operator bio: local desert team, driver experience, languages, years operating.
- Add booking policy, cancellation terms, payment terms, and pickup details.
- Add original route photos, vehicle photos, and camp photos.

Then pursue links/citations:

- Google Business Profile.
- Tripadvisor listing.
- Local tourism directories.
- Morocco travel blogs.
- Hotel/riad partner pages in Marrakech.
- Travel forums only where genuinely helpful, never spammy.
